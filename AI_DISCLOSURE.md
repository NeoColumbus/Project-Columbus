# AI Disclosure

Full City Columbus is openly AI-collaborated. Website building and maintenance are allowed AI uses. AI assistance is not a credential, a source, a resident, or evidence of public support. It also does not make every artifact a disposable placeholder.

## Artifact Inventory

| Artifact | Current disclosure | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Website code | AI-assisted | Implementation, tests, and maintenance use coding agents; review and deployment remain project responsibilities. |
| Website copy | AI-assisted | Developed through human direction and iteration with AI. Institutional voice is not a claim of a large staff. |
| Research notes | AI-assisted | Retrieval and drafting use AI; cite the underlying source, distinguish interpretation, and disclose incomplete checks. |
| Policy drafts | AI-assisted | Project proposals, not adopted law, professional advice, or endorsements. |
| Posters and stickers | Mixed | Human-directed, AI-assisted design/code and iteration; individual image provenance belongs in image credits. |
| Flag / visual identity | AI-assisted | Developed through human selection and AI-assisted iteration; not an official city flag. |
| COTA computational work | Separate repository; provenance not independently audited here | Consult its code and audit trail. Public code does not establish validated findings or a complete authorship inventory. |
| Photography | Mixed by asset | Original photographs retain their named creators/licenses; photo-based AI edits are identified. See image credits. No generated image is field evidence. |
| Submitted community evidence | Contributor-supplied; no blanket authorship claim | Record provenance and alterations during review. No fabricated participation or invented photographs. |

This inventory describes known project process, not an assertion that every file has a complete authorship record. A missing attribution is a correction to make, not a reason to guess. [Image credits](site/assets/IMAGE_CREDITS.md) give asset-specific credits and licenses.

## Boundaries

AI may draft, research, implement, test, summarize, and screen risk. It may not impersonate residents, manufacture consensus, invent sources, fabricate field evidence, or independently promote leads to PROOF. Historians, riders, neighborhood residents, designers, public servants, and living Native voices cannot be replaced by generated text.

The optional intake classifier is disabled unless explicitly configured. It screens risk only; it does not decide truth. Deterministic screening and human publication review remain required.

## Correct This Inventory

Open a [research correction](https://github.com/NeoColumbus/Project-Columbus/issues/new?template=research-correction.md) identifying the artifact, the inaccurate disclosure, and supporting provenance. Do not include private information or credentials. Publication and corrections remain the project's responsibility.
