02 / field card

Put a place on record.

No login at the front door. Make the card, copy it, share it, then send it upstream when the line is sharp enough.

LEAD: place, missing piece, one line. No photo link required. PROOF: the same claim with checkable evidence, checked by a maintainer before publication. Sending a card does not put it on the proof wall.

Reports are screened before anything is published. Submissions begin as leads. Evidence is checked before a lead becomes proof. Keep personal details and secrets out; no visitor profile is created.
